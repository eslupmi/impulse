# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the project!

At the moment, we are **not accepting code contributions or pull requests** due to licensing considerations.

However, we welcome issues and suggestions.

## How you can help

### 🐛 Bugs and issues

Report bugs and other issues by [opening a GitHub issue](https://github.com/eslupmi/impulse/issues/new). Include as much information as possible:

- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Relevant logs or screenshots
- Your environment and version of the project

### 💡 Ideas and suggestions

Share ideas and suggestions in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/eslupmi/discussions).

### 💬 Feedback

For other questions and feedback, write to [support@impulse.bot](mailto:support@impulse.bot).

## Thank you

We appreciate your understanding and thank you for helping us improve the project by reporting issues and sharing your ideas!
